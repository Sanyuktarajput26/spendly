Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. Basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, may be collected automatically to help us maintain the service.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly's core functionality — letting you log expenses, view spending summaries, and manage your account. Your email address may be used to send you important account-related notices. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data will be removed from our systems within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal or security reasons.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may use trusted third-party services for infrastructure needs such as hosting. Any such providers are only given access to the data necessary to perform their function and are expected to safeguard it accordingly.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
